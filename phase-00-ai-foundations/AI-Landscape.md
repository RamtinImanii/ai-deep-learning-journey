# AI Landscape

## Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is the broad field of building systems that can perform tasks that normally require human-like intelligence.

### Machine Learning

Machine Learning allows systems to learn patterns from data.

### Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a subfield of Machine Learning based mainly on neural networks with multiple layers.

### Computer Vision

Computer Vision focuses on understanding and analyzing images and videos.

### Generative AI

Generative AI focuses on generating new content such as text, images, audio, video, and code.

### Large Language Models

LLMs are large neural language models designed to understand and generate natural language.

### AI Agents

AI Agents are systems that can pursue goals through planning, tool use, actions, and observations.
