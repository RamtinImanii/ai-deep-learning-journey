# AI Landscape

## 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI is a general concept that refers to systems that can perform intelligent tasks. These systems can perform tasks that usually require human intelligence, such as decision-making, recognition, prediction, and problem-solving.

---

## 2. Machine Learning (ML)

ML is one of the most important approaches in AI. Instead of programming all the decision-making rules manually, we build a model that learns patterns from data and uses them to make predictions or decisions.

---

## 3. Deep Learning (DL)

DL is a subfield of ML that is based on multi-layer neural networks (Deep Neural Networks). It is especially useful for learning complex patterns from large amounts of data.

---

## 4. Neural Networks

Neural Networks are models made of units called neurons (artificial neurons). They are inspired by the human nervous system. By connecting many neurons together, these models can learn and extract complex patterns from data.

---

## 5. Computer Vision (CV)

Computer Vision is a field of AI that enables computers to analyze images and videos and extract useful information from them.

### Examples

- **Self-driving cars:** Understanding the environment around the vehicle and detecting pedestrians, animals, other vehicles, and other objects that can affect driving.
- **Surveillance cameras:** Detecting people and objects and identifying certain activities or unusual behaviors.
- **Robots:** Understanding their surrounding environment and detecting and recognizing different objects.

---

## 6. Generative AI

Generative AI is a field of AI that focuses on generating new content based on learned patterns. It can generate different types of content, such as text, images, audio, video, and code.

---

## 7. Large Language Models (LLMs)

An LLM is a large language model designed to process language, learn patterns in text, and generate human-like text.

---

## 8. AI Agents

AI Agents are systems that can work toward a specific goal. They can analyze a situation, make a plan, use tools, perform multiple steps, evaluate the results, and change their approach based on those results.
